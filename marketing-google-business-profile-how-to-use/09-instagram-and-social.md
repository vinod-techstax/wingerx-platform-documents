# Instagram & Social Media Marketing

The **Instagram** and **Social Media** tabs extend Google Business Profile marketing into visual and social discovery channels, allowing marketing teams to repurpose local GBP content across Instagram, Facebook, and linked multi-channel feeds.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Instagram** tab (or click the **Social Media** tab).

---

## 1. Overview
Local customers discover businesses through both Google Search/Maps and social visual discovery. Coordinating content between GBP and social channels compounds brand recognition and engagement.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Instagram Tab
* **Connected Account:** Displays the linked Instagram Business account profile, handle, follower count, and bio.
* **Feed Preview:** Visual grid view of published and scheduled Instagram posts.
* **Repurpose from GBP:**
  * Convert high-performing Smart Posts or customer review testimonials into square (1:1) or story (9:16) image carousels with hashtags.
* **Publishing / Scheduling:**
  * Schedule reels, single photos, or carousels with scheduled publishing timestamps.

---

### B. Social Media Hub (Cross-Navigation)
* In the left sidebar, clicking the **Social Media** tab opens the dedicated Social Media Marketing module ('https://app.wingerx.com/social-media-marketing?from=marketing').
* Supports multi-platform management across Facebook Pages, LinkedIn, and Instagram.
