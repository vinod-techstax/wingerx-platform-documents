# Prompts & Governance Rules

The **Prompts & Rules** tab allows marketing engineers and account managers to fine-tune system prompts, customize generation logic for each content format, and enforce strict content boundaries across the AI pipeline.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Prompts & Rules** tab.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
- Workspace selected.
- Operator or Administrator permissions to adjust prompt templates.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Prompt Templates by Feature
Customize the underlying system instructions for each AI feature:
* **Google Update Post Prompt:** Defines structure, character limits (optimal 150–300 words), emoji limits (max 2–3), and CTA phrasing.
* **Review Reply Prompt:** Directs the AI on handling 5-star praise vs. 1-star complaints.
* **Blog Article Prompt:** Controls H2/H3 outline depth, FAQ formatting, and keyword density.
* **Notification Message Prompt:** Manages variable injection and tone for WhatsApp/Email updates.

---

### B. Formatting & Output Rules
* **Max Length Restrictions:** Ensures post copy does not get truncated on mobile Google Search.
* **Hashtag Policy:** Google Business Profile does not benefit from hashtag stuffing; limit to 2–3 hyper-local tags (e.g., `#BangaloreDentist`).
* **Phone & URL Placement:** Defines whether URLs should be placed in the body or strictly through the native Google CTA button.
