# Brand Identity & Brand Kit

The **Brand Identity** tab (also called **Brand Kit**) establishes the creative guidelines, visual assets, voice attributes, and compliance restrictions that govern all automated content generated for a business location.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Brand Identity** tab.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
- A location must be selected.
- Complete this setup before generating posts, blogs, or review replies.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Core Brand Elements
* **Brand Name & Tagline:** Official legal business name and consumer-facing slogan (e.g., *"Precision Care, Honest Prices"*).
* **Target Audience:** Who the business serves (e.g., *"Local families, vehicle owners within 10km radius"*).
* **Brand Values:** Core pillars that should resonate in all messaging (e.g., *"Transparency, Speed, Certified Experts"*).

---

### B. Visual Identity (Colors & Logo)
* **Primary Color:** Hex code (e.g., `#2563EB`) used for branded cards, banners, and overlays.
* **Secondary / Accent Color:** Complementary color for buttons and highlights.
* **Brand Logo:** High-res vector or PNG logo asset.

---

### C. Voice & Tone Presets
Select or customize the brand's writing personality:
* `Professional & Authoritative` (clinics, legal, financial services)
* `Warm & Friendly` (cafes, salons, wellness spas)
* `Energetic & Action-Oriented` (gyms, auto detailing, adventure sports)
* `Direct & Problem-Solving` (emergency plumbers, locksmiths, repair services)

---

### D. Prohibited Claims & Safety Restrictions (Crucial)
Enter explicit negative rules that the AI must **never** write:
* Examples:
  * *"Never guarantee 100% cure or pain-free outcomes."*
  * *"Do not mention specific pricing without promotional code approval."*
  * *"Do not disparage or mention competitor brand names."*
* The governance engine scans every generated draft against these rules; violations are automatically blocked or flagged as **High Risk** in the **Approval Queue** tab.
