# AI Usage Logs & Credit Tracking

The **AI Usage Logs** tab provides detailed accounting and visibility into AI credit consumption, token usage, model choices, and operational cost across all generative features in the marketing workspace.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **AI Usage Logs** tab.

---

## 1. Why Track AI Usage
* **Cost Control:** Monitor credit usage per client location.
* **Agency Billing:** Accurately reconcile AI expenditures against client retainers.
* **Performance Analysis:** Evaluate prompt efficiency and output token volume.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Credit Balance & Consumption Cards
* **Available Credits:** Current remaining token/credit quota for the workspace.
* **Monthly Consumed:** Total credits burned in the active calendar month.
* **Top Consuming Feature:** Highlights whether Smart Posts, Blog Articles, or Review Replies used the most credits.

---

### B. Usage Breakdown Table
Each generation event is logged with:
* **Timestamp:** Date and time of generation.
* **Feature:** `SMART_POST`, `REVIEW_REPLY`, `BLOG_ARTICLE`, `GBP_AUDIT_RECOMMENDATIONS`.
* **Location:** Associated business location.
* **Prompt Tokens & Completion Tokens:** Exact LLM token count.
* **Model Used:** Underlying model (e.g., `gemini-1.5-pro`, `gemini-1.5-flash`).
* **Status:** `Success` or `Failed`.
