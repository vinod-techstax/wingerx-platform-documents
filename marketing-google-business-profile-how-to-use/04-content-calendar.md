# Content Calendar & Scheduling Guide

The **Content Calendar** tab provides an interactive calendar interface to plan, schedule, visualize, and balance content cadence across Google Business Profile updates, offers, and events.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Calendar** tab.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
- A location must be selected.
- Posts can be planned in draft state or scheduled for automated publishing.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Calendar Views
* **Month View:** High-level birds-eye view of post density across the 30-day cycle.
* **Week View:** Detailed hourly/daily layout for verifying publication time slots (e.g., Tuesday 10:00 AM, Thursday 2:00 PM).
* **Navigation Controls:** Switch between months and jump to "Today".

---

### B. Post Status Color Coding
Each event in the calendar reflects its current lifecycle state:
* 🟢 **Published:** Confirmed live on the client's Google Business Profile.
* 🟡 **Scheduled / Approved:** Approved by the marketing operator and queued for automated publication.
* 🔵 **Draft / Pending Approval:** Generated by AI or team members, waiting for review in the **Approval Queue** tab.
* ⚪ **Planned / Idea:** Placeholder slot for future content.

---

### C. Adding a Post Directly from the Calendar
1. Click on any target calendar day.
2. Select **"Add New Post"**.
3. Choose the post type:
   * **What's New / Standard Update:** General business update, service spotlight, or team highlight.
   * **Offer / Promo:** Time-sensitive promotion with coupon code and validity period.
   * **Event:** Upcoming local event or webinar with start and end times.
4. Input copy or click **"Generate with AI"** to draft copy based on target keywords.
5. Select a scheduled date and time.
6. Click **Save Draft** or **Submit to Approval Queue** (reviewed in the **Approval Queue** tab).

---

### D. Best Practices for GBP Posting Cadence
* **Target Cadence:** 2 to 3 posts per week (minimum 8–10 posts per month).
* **Peak Times:** Local businesses experience highest discovery engagement mid-morning on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
* **Freshness Signals:** Continuous calendar activity signals to Google's ranking algorithm that the business is open, active, and responsive.
