# Media Library & GBP Photo Assets

The **Media Library** tab centralizes all digital assets (photos, graphics, infographics, before-and-after comparisons) for a Google Business Profile location. Maintaining a frequent upload cadence of high-quality photos is one of the highest-weighted signals for local Google ranking.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Media Library** tab.

---

## 1. Why Photos Influence GBP Visibility
* Google official documentation states that businesses with 100+ photos receive **42% more requests for driving directions** and **35% more clicks through to their website**.
* High photo cadence (uploading at least 4–6 photos monthly) signals that the physical business is thriving and legitimate.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Asset Categories
When uploading assets, tag them with Google Business Profile categories:
* **Interior:** Reception, customer seating, exam rooms, work bays, showroom floors.
* **Exterior:** Street entrance, building facade, parking availability, daytime and evening signage.
* **Team & Staff:** Key management, technicians, doctors, customer service staff at work.
* **Products & Services:** Physical merchandise, food/menu items, completed work, before-and-after results.
* **Cover & Logo:** Square brand logo (min 720x720 px) and landscape cover photo (1024x576 px).

---

### B. Uploading and Tagging Media
1. In the left sidebar, click the **Media Library** tab.
2. Click **"Upload Media"** or drag files directly into the drop zone.
3. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP (under 10MB per file).
4. Assign:
   * **Category Tag:** Interior, Exterior, Team, Product, Other.
   * **Asset Title & Caption:** Descriptive alt-text containing local keywords.
5. Click **"Save to Library"**.

---

### C. Attaching Media to Content
* In **Smart Posts** (click the **Smart Posts** tab), click **"Select from Media Library"** to attach existing brand assets without re-uploading.
* In **Blog Articles** (click the **Blog Articles** tab), select header and inline image assets.
