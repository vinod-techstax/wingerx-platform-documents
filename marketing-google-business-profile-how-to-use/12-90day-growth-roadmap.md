# 90-Day GBP Growth Roadmap (Tasks)

The **Tasks** tab presents the **90-Day GBP Growth Roadmap**, an automated, lifecycle-aware growth engine that structures Google Business Profile optimization into three 30-day phases. Every task is auto-seeded from real audit findings and linked directly to measurable local SEO milestones.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Tasks** tab.

---

## 1. Roadmap Architecture: The 3 Phases

Rather than a generic to-do list, tasks are organized into three distinct operational phases:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1 to 30)                                    │
│  Objective: Fix critical profile omissions, establish baseline data.  │
│  Key Focus: Missing phone, categories, hours, services, attributes,   │
│             initial photo uploads, verifying profile completeness.     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  PHASE 2: ENGAGEMENT (Days 31 to 60)                                   │
│  Objective: Build local search momentum, trust, and active cadence.    │
│  Key Focus: Review reply cadence, customer Q&A setup, weekly Google   │
│             updates, photo additions, special seasonal hours.          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  PHASE 3: AUTHORITY (Days 61 to 90)                                    │
│  Objective: Expand local 3-pack rankings, competitive parity, growth.  │
│  Key Focus: High-intent keyword targeting, citation alignment,         │
│             competitor parity, long-form content, customer calls.      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Real-Time GBP Metrics Summary Bar
Positioned prominently across the top of the roadmap, displaying real persisted database metrics:
* **Total Reviews & Avg Rating:** e.g., `4.8 ★ (124 reviews)`
* **30-Day Posts Published:** Count of active posts published on Google in the last 30 days (benchmark: 8+).
* **Active Tracked Keywords:** Number of approved keywords monitored for rank improvements.
* **Audit Health Score:** Overall profile health score from the latest audit (e.g., `78/100`).
* **Overall Phase Progress:** Total tasks completed vs. remaining across all 3 phases.

---

### B. Phase Columns & Progress Bars
Each phase displays:
* **Phase Badge & Due Date:** Calculated dynamically from location creation (e.g., `Phase 1 • Due Nov 30, 2026`).
* **Progress Bar:** Percentage of tasks completed in that specific phase.
* **Task Counter:** e.g., `3 of 5 Completed`.

---

### C. Rich Task Cards
Every task card provides clear, actionable guidance:
* **Status Badge:** `TODO` (blue), `IN_PROGRESS` (amber), `WAITING_APPROVAL` (purple), `COMPLETED` (green), `SKIPPED` (gray).
* **Task Type Icon & Action Code:** Identifies the exact operation (e.g., `PROFILE_UPDATE`, `POST_PUBLISH`, `PHOTO_UPLOAD`, `REVIEW_REPLY`).
* **Title & Rich Description:** Factual, step-by-step instructions on what must be done.
* **Why & Expected Impact:** Explains the business rationale (e.g., *"Why: Google algorithms reward profiles with 10+ photos with 42% more direction requests. Expected Impact: +15% discovery views"*).
* **Target Due Date:** Exact calendar due date based on phase offset.
* **Action Buttons:**
  * **"Complete":** Marks the task finished with optional completion notes and proof link.
  * **"Skip":** Archives the task with an optional reason if not applicable to this client.

---

### D. "Regenerate Roadmap" Feature
Located at the top right of the roadmap:
* Clicking **"Regenerate Roadmap"** triggers the recommendation engine to re-scan all audit findings and create missing tasks for newly identified issues.
* Existing completed or in-progress tasks are preserved (duplicate protection prevents re-creating completed work).

---

### E. Completed & Skipped Tasks Archive
At the bottom of the roadmap, an expandable accordion preserves completed and skipped tasks for client audit reports and historical proof of work.
