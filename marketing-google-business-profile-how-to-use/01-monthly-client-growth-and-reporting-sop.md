# Monthly Client Growth & Recurring Reporting SOP

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) details the essential recurring actions, cadence checks, and reporting obligations that account managers must perform every month for each client location to guarantee measurable business growth and high client retention.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Monthly Report** tab.

---

## 1. The Monthly Growth Cycle at a Glance

Every month follows a structured 4-week cadence:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  WEEK 1 (Days 1–7):   MONTH-END REPORTING & CLIENT DELIVERY            │
│  • Aggregate metrics (Views, Calls, Clicks, Directions)                │
│  • Generate Monthly Growth Report (YYYY-MM) in "Monthly Report" tab    │
│  • Deliver Report Summary to client via WhatsApp (MSG_13) & Email      │
│  • Host/Schedule 15-minute Monthly Growth Review with client           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WEEK 2 (Days 8–14):  CONTENT CALENDAR & POST SCHEDULING               │
│  • Plan 8–12 GBP Posts in "Calendar" & "Smart Posts"                   │
│  • Upload fresh photo assets to "Media Library" (Exterior/Interior)    │
│  • Review & approve queued posts in "Approval Queue"                   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WEEK 3 (Days 15–21): REPUTATION & REVIEW ACCELERATION                 │
│  • Ingest and respond to all pending reviews in "Review Replies"       │
│  • Verify 100% response rate with positive, keyword-rich replies       │
│  • Review keyword rank tracking in "Keywords"                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  WEEK 4 (Days 22–30): ROADMAP ADVANCEMENT & RE-AUDIT                   │
│  • Advance phase tasks in "Tasks" (90-Day GBP Growth Roadmap)          │
│  • Mark completed tasks and review Phase KPI progress                  │
│  • Run full GBP Audit to measure monthly score increase                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 2. Week 1: Monthly Reporting & Client Delivery (Mandatory)

### Step 1: Aggregate Monthly Performance Metrics
1. Select the client's location from the top location dropdown.
2. In the left sidebar, click **GBP Integrations** tab → **Performance Metrics** (or view the Metrics section).
3. If the profile is connected via Google OAuth, the system syncs metrics automatically from Google Insights API.
4. If managing a manual/Places location, enter the monthly totals from the client's Google Maps performance dashboard:
   * **Search & Maps Impressions** (Profile views)
   * **Website Clicks**
   * **Phone Calls**
   * **Direction Requests**
   * **Bookings / Messaging Inquiries** (if applicable)
5. Save the aggregated metrics for the previous month (format: `YYYY-MM`, e.g., `2026-10`).

---

### Step 2: Generate the Monthly Growth Report
1. In the left sidebar, click the **Monthly Report** tab.
2. At the top of the screen, select the target reporting month (e.g., `2026-10`).
3. Click **Generate / Refresh Report**.
4. The system automatically compiles:
   * **GBP Posts Published:** Total verified posts published during the month.
   * **Reviews Answered:** Total replies published vs. received.
   * **Search Views:** Impressions and search discovery trend.
   * **Compliance Score:** Policy check rating (target: 100%).
   * **Customer Actions:** Breakdown of Calls, Website Visits, and Directions.
   * **Keyword Status:** Active ranking target keywords vs. suggested ideas.
   * **Audit Findings Resolved:** Issues fixed during the month.

---

### Step 3: Send Report to Client via WhatsApp & Email
Once the report is reviewed, notify the client using the pre-formatted **Monthly Report Summary** templates:

#### A. Send via WhatsApp (MSG_13):
1. Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page, click **Notifications** tab, and switch to **Manual Send**.
2. Scroll to the **Reports** section and select **MSG_13 — Monthly Report Summary**.
3. Click **View & Send**.
4. Verify variables:
   * `{{first_name}}`: Client name
   * `{{month_name}}`: e.g., "October 2026"
   * `{{profile_views}}`: Total impressions
   * `{{customer_actions}}`: Combined calls + directions + clicks
   * `{{posts_published}}`: Number of Google posts published
   * `{{reviews_handled}}`: Number of reviews answered
   * `{{report_link}}`: Link to the online report
5. Click **Send WhatsApp Message**.

```text
Hi {{first_name}} 📊

Here is your Monthly Growth Report for {{month_name}} for {{business_name}}!

📈 Performance Highlights:
• Profile Views: {{profile_views}}
• Customer Inquiries & Actions: {{customer_actions}}
• GBP Posts Published: {{posts_published}}
• Reviews Answered: {{reviews_handled}} (100% response rate)

👉 View your full interactive report:
{{report_link}}

Let us know if you have any questions or upcoming promotions!
```

#### B. Send via Email (EMAIL_13):
1. In the **Notifications** tab, switch to **Email Notifications** → **Manual Send**.
2. Select **EMAIL_13 — Monthly Marketing Report Summary**.
3. Click **View & Send** → **Send Email**.

## 3. Week 2: Content Planning & Fresh Media Cadence

High GBP visibility requires consistent activity (at least 2–3 updates per week):

1. **Content Calendar:** Click the **Calendar** tab.
   * Inspect the upcoming month's calendar.
   * Plan 8 to 12 Google updates across the month (Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays perform best for local discovery).
2. **Smart Posts:** Click the **Smart Posts** tab.
   * Generate 4 **Standard Updates** highlighting core services and customer value.
   * Generate 2 **Offer Posts** with promo codes, seasonal discounts, or consultation incentives.
   * Generate 2 **Service Highlights** embedding primary and secondary keywords.
3. **Media Library:** Click the **Media Library** tab.
   * Request 4–6 high-resolution photos from the client every month.
   * Upload and tag them under:
     * `Interior` (storefront, reception, waiting lounge)
     * `Exterior` (entrance, signage, parking)
     * `Team & Staff` (technicians, doctors, customer reps)
     * `Work & Products` (before/after, menu items, equipment)
   * Attach relevant photos to the generated Smart Posts.
4. **Approval Queue:** Click the **Approval Queue** tab.
   * Review each generated post.
   * Check brand voice, grammar, and CTA link.
   * Click **Approve**. (For Places locations, click **Complete Manually** after posting).

---

## 4. Week 3: Reputation & Review Management

Google algorithms heavily prioritize listings with frequent, keyword-rich review replies:

1. **Review Replies:** Click the **Review Replies** tab.
   * Check for any unreplied reviews received in the past 30 days.
   * Generate AI replies using the appropriate tone:
     * **5-Star Reviews:** Enthusiastic gratitude, mentioning the specific service and location name.
     * **3-4 Star Reviews:** Professional appreciation with open offer to improve.
     * **1-2 Star Reviews:** Empathetic, de-escalating response with offline contact email/phone.
   * Approve all pending replies so the response rate remains **100%**.
2. **Review Generation Prompt:**
   * If review count is low, suggest that the client send their Google Review Shortlink to recent satisfied customers via SMS/WhatsApp.

---

## 5. Week 4: 90-Day Roadmap Progression & Monthly Re-Audit

### 1. Advance the 90-Day GBP Growth Roadmap
1. In the sidebar, click the **Tasks** tab (90-Day GBP Growth Roadmap).
2. Review the active phase:
   * **Month 1 (Days 1–30): Phase 1 — Foundation**
     * Ensure Phone, Categories, Special Hours, Services, and Initial Photos are completed.
   * **Month 2 (Days 31–60): Phase 2 — Engagement**
     * Ensure Review cadence, Google Posts cadence, Q&A setup, and Customer Engagement are active.
   * **Month 3 (Days 61–90): Phase 3 — Authority**
     * Focus on Competitive Parity, Authority Citations, and High-Intent Keyword rankings.
3. On tasks that your team completed during the month:
   * Click **Complete** on the task card.
   * Provide optional completion notes (e.g., "Updated holiday hours for Diwali/Thanksgiving").
4. If new audit findings require fresh tasks:
   * Click **Regenerate Roadmap** at the top right to incorporate newly discovered opportunities.

---

### 2. Run the Monthly GBP Re-Audit
1. In the sidebar, click the **GBP Integrations** tab → **Audits**.
2. Click **Run Audit**.
3. Compare the new score against the previous month:
   * Target: **+10 to +15 points score increase** per month until reaching `85+/100`.
   * Check the Resolved Findings list to verify progress.
