# GBP Integrations, Location Settings & Audits

The **GBP Integrations** tab manages Google account connections, location records, detailed business settings, Google Places sync, and on-demand GBP Health Audits.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **GBP Integrations** tab.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
- Workspace selected.
- Google OAuth credentials or Google Places API access.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Location Connection & Onboarding
* **Connect Google Account (OAuth):** Connects with Google Business Profile APIs, enabling automated post publishing, review replies, and live metric synchronization.
* **Google Places Search:** Allows adding businesses without direct Google Account ownership. Ideal for agency lead generation, audits, and manual management workflows.

---

### B. Location Settings & Business Context
Click **Edit Location** or **Location Settings** to maintain profile fidelity:
* **Business Information:** Official name, phone number (E.164 format), website URL, physical address.
* **Categories:** 1 Primary Category (most impactful for ranking) and up to 9 Secondary Categories.
* **Hours of Operation:** Regular weekly schedule and holiday/special hours.
* **Services & Attributes:** Detailed service list with descriptions and attribute flags (e.g., Wheelchair Accessible, Free Wi-Fi, Outdoor Seating).

---

### C. Location Audits & Health Scoring
1. Under the **Audits** section, click **"Run Audit"**.
2. The audit engine evaluates 12+ profile parameters:
   * Profile completeness & missing fields
   * Photo count and upload cadence
   * Review volume and average rating
   * Unreplied reviews count
   * Special hours and attributes setup
   * Google Q&A presence
3. **Audit Results:**
   * **Overall Health Score (0–100):** Visual gauge of listing strength.
   * **Critical Findings:** Must-fix issues harming visibility.
   * **Growth Opportunities:** High-leverage optimizations.
   * **Audit Hook:** Completing an audit automatically updates the **Tasks** tab (90-Day Growth Roadmap)!
