# Review Replies & Reputation Management

The **Review Replies** tab streamlines Google customer review monitoring, sentiment analysis, AI-assisted response generation, and reply publishing to maximize client reputation and local map pack rankings.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Review Replies** tab.

---

## 1. Why Review Responses Matter for GBP Ranking
Google algorithms directly correlate review responsiveness with local ranking authority:
* **Ranking Signal:** Businesses with a 100% response rate rank higher in the local 3-pack.
* **Customer Trust:** 88% of consumers prioritize businesses that respond to all customer feedback.
* **SEO Keyword Embedding:** Professional replies naturally mention services and locations (e.g., *"Thank you for trusting our car repair center in Bangalore..."*), reinforcing relevance signals.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Reviews List & Sentiment Filtering
* **Filter Tabs:** All Reviews, Unreplied Reviews, 5-Star Reviews, Critical (1–3 Stars).
* **Card Details:** Reviewer name, star rating, review date, review text, Google reply status (`Pending`, `Replied`).

---

### B. Generating AI Review Replies
1. Select an unreplied review from the list.
2. Click **"Generate Reply"**.
3. Choose the response strategy / tone:
   * **Enthusiastic (5-Star):** Grateful, warm, reinforces the specific service mentioned, invites them back.
   * **Professional & Solution-Oriented (3-4 Star):** Acknowledges positive aspects, politely addresses minor issues, offers direct manager contact.
   * **Empathetic & De-escalating (1-2 Star):** Apologetic without admitting legal liability, takes the conversation offline immediately with email/phone.
4. Review the generated response in the text editor.
5. Click **"Approve & Publish"** (or **"Submit to Approval Queue"** if human review is required in the **Approval Queue** tab).

---

### C. Manual Completion for Places Locations
For Google Places managed locations without OAuth publishing permissions:
1. Copy the approved reply text.
2. Open the business listing in Google Maps / Google Search.
3. Click **"Reply"** on the customer's review and paste the response.
4. Return to WingerX and click **"Mark as Replied Manually"**.
