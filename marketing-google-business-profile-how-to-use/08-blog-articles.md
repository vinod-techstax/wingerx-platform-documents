# Blog Articles & Local Authority Content

The **Blog Articles** tab manages long-form SEO articles designed to establish local topical authority, answer high-intent customer search queries, and drive organic referral traffic directly to the client's Google Business Profile and website.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Blog Articles** tab.

---

## 1. Why Blog Articles Support GBP Ranking
While Google Posts provide short, ephemeral social updates, long-form blog articles (800–1,500 words):
* Target long-tail local search queries (e.g., *"Best preventive dental care clinic in Indiranagar Bangalore"*).
* Provide backlink and citation anchors that reinforce local business relevance.
* Can be summarized into bite-sized Google Posts and WhatsApp newsletters.

---

## 2. Key Components & How to Use Them

### A. Articles List & Status Lifecycle
* **Draft:** Article outline and copy generated, undergoing editorial polish.
* **Pending Approval:** Submitted to client or manager for sign-off.
* **Approved:** Ready for publication to client's CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Custom Next.js).
* **Published:** Live with confirmed public canonical URL.

---

### B. Generating a Blog Article with AI
1. In the left sidebar, click the **Blog Articles** tab.
2. Click **"New Blog Article"** (or **"Generate with AI"**).
3. Fill in:
   * **Target Topic / Title:** e.g., "The Complete Guide to Choosing Commercial HVAC Maintenance in Dallas".
   * **Target Keywords:** Select 2–4 approved primary and secondary keywords.
   * **Target Word Count:** 800, 1,200, or 1,500 words.
   * **Target Audience:** Residential homeowners, corporate facility managers, etc.
4. Click **"Generate Outline & Draft"**.
5. The AI builds:
   * SEO Title & Meta Description.
   * Structured H2 and H3 subheadings.
   * Comprehensive body copy with localized references.
   * FAQ section targeting Google People Also Ask (PAA).
   * Concluding Call to Action linking to the client's GBP profile.
6. Review, refine in the markdown editor, and submit to the **Approval Queue** tab.
