# Smart Posts AI Generation Guide

The **Smart Posts** tab allows marketing managers to generate, edit, organize, and publish high-converting Google Business Profile update posts powered by domain-specific AI models that respect brand voice, prohibited claims, and local SEO keywords.

> **Navigation:** Go to https://app.wingerx.com/marketing page and click the **Smart Posts** tab.

---

## 1. Prerequisites
- A location must be selected.
- Brand Identity (Voice, Prohibited Claims) should be configured in the **Brand Identity** tab.
- At least 2–3 active keywords should be approved in **Keywords**.

---

## 2. Post Types Supported by Google Business Profile

| Post Type | Purpose | Key Attributes |
|---|---|---|
| **What's New (Standard)** | General business updates, customer education, behind-the-scenes. | Text (up to 1,500 chars), 1 Image, Call-To-Action (CTA) button. |
| **Offer / Promotion** | Special discounts, seasonal sales, first-time customer promos. | Title, Start/End dates, Coupon Code, Terms & Conditions, Redeem CTA. |
| **Event** | Workshops, open houses, product launches, community drives. | Event Title, Start/End dates & times, Photo, Event Details, RSVP CTA. |
| **Service Spotlight** | Deep dive into a specific business service or specialty. | Keywords embedded naturally, benefit highlights, "Book" CTA. |

---

## 3. How to Generate a Smart Post with AI

1. In the left sidebar, click the **Smart Posts** tab.
2. Click the **"Generate Post"** button (or **"New Post"**).
3. In the post generation modal:
   * **Target Topic / Theme:** Enter a prompt or select a pre-configured theme (e.g., "Monsoon car servicing tips" or "Dental teeth whitening discount").
   * **Post Type:** Select Standard, Offer, or Event.
   * **Keywords to Target:** Select 1–3 target keywords from your approved keywords list.
   * **Tone / Voice:** Inherits from Brand Identity (or override: Professional, Friendly, Urgent).
   * **Call To Action (CTA):** Choose button type (`Learn More`, `Book`, `Call Now`, `Order Online`).
   * **CTA Link:** Enter the destination URL (e.g., appointment booking page).
4. Click **"Generate Draft"**.
5. The AI creates a compliant draft incorporating:
   * Engaging hook
   * Keyword-rich body copy
   * Local business hashtags (2–3 max)
   * CTA button text and link
6. Review and edit the generated copy.
7. Click **"Attach Media"** to pick a photo from the **Media Library** tab.
8. Click **"Send to Approval Queue"** (or schedule in the **Calendar** tab).

---

## 4. Manual Completion for Places Locations

For non-API (Google Places) managed profiles:
1. Copy the approved copy and download the attached image.
2. Go to Google Maps or Google Search and open the client's GBP manager.
3. Click **"Add Update"** → paste copy, upload image, add button link.
4. Return to WingerX and click **"Complete Manually"** in the **Approval Queue** tab to mark the post as published.
